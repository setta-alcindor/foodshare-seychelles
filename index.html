Our Work

Connecting Surplus to Need

FoodShare Seychelles is a centralized digital logistics platform dedicated to eliminating food waste and combating food insecurity. We partner with local businesses, supermarkets, and individuals to redirect high-quality, surplus food directly to the communities and families that need it most.
