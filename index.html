Our Work

Connecting Surplus to Need

FoodShare Seychelles is a centralized digital logistics platform dedicated to eliminating food waste and combating food insecurity. We partner with local businesses, supermarkets, and individuals to redirect high-quality, surplus food directly to the communities and families that need it most.

The Pillars of FoodShare

Uncompromising Food Safety

Quality and dignity go hand in hand. Every donation passes through our strict digital inventory system to track best-by dates, allergen information, and storage requirements.

Our staff and volunteers follow rigorous protocols to ensure that all fresh produce, canned goods, and prepared meals are transported and distributed safely. We believe surplus food should always mean safe, high-quality food.

Read our Safety Guidelines

Digital Innovation

Charity meets modern logistics. We have engineered a custom platform that connects donors directly to our fleet, and our fleet directly to clients in real-time.

Our automated dashboards manage everything from driver routing to instant WhatsApp notifications for client pickups. By digitizing the donation pipeline, we reduce response times and drastically minimize the risk of food spoiling in transit.

Explore our Tech Infrastructure

A Nationwide Movement

FoodShare is not just an organization; it is a collaborative community effort. Our success is driven by the hundreds of volunteers, local farmers, corporate donors, and community leaders who give their time and resources.

Together, we are shifting the culture around food waste in Seychelles, building a sustainable ecosystem where communities take care of each other.
