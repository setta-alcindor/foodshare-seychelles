Privacy Policy

How we collect, utilize, and cryptographically protect your information within the FoodShare network.

Last Updated: June 4, 2026
Effective Date: Upon account creation or use of FoodShare Seychelles services.

1 Information We Collect

To ensure safe and efficient food redistribution, we collect specific data when you register an account, log a donation, or claim a food package. This includes:

  • Identity Data: First name, last name, and organizational affiliations.
  • Contact Data: Email addresses and mobile telephone numbers.
  • Logistics Data: Physical delivery addresses and routing instructions required for our fleet dispatch.
  • Application Data: Resumes, availability schedules, and background check consent logs for volunteers.

2 How We Use Your Information

FoodShare Seychelles strictly limits the use of your data to operational necessity. We utilize your information to:

  • Authenticate your secure access to the client, volunteer, or administrative dashboards.
  • Coordinate physical pick-ups and drop-offs between donors and recipients.
  • Communicate critical account updates, support ticket resolutions, and safety recalls.
  • Conduct necessary background verifications for volunteers interacting with vulnerable communities.

3 Data Security & Protection

We employ military-grade, cloud-based security architectures. All personal data is housed in isolated relational databases enforced by Row Level Security (RLS) constraints. This guarantees that your profile information, donation history, and claims records are cryptographically locked and invisible to unauthorized network nodes.

4 Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or lease your personal information to third parties. Data is only shared under the following strict conditions:

  • Internal Logistics: Address and contact data is temporarily routed to our vetted driver fleet solely for the purpose of completing a scheduled delivery or pickup.
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose information if required by Seychellois law, judicial proceedings, or to protect the absolute safety of our community network.

5 Your Rights & Controls

You maintain full control over your digital footprint within our ecosystem. Through your authenticated Client Dashboard, you may:

  • Review and update your contact parameters and physical logistics addresses at any time.
  • Review your historical logs of donations, claims, and volunteer applications.
  • Request a complete deletion of your account and associated identifying data by contacting our compliance desk.

Data Privacy Inquiries

If you have questions regarding our data handling or wish to enact a data removal request, please contact our administrative desk.
